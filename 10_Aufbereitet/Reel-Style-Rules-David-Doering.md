## Details
**Beobachtet im Bild**
- Rot: kräftiges Band/Schleife, rotes Hemd.
- Weiß: Anzug, Titelschrift „DAVID DÖRING".
- Blau: Pinselstrich hinter dem Mittel-Cover.
- Fast schwarz: Hintergrund Halleluja-Cover.
- Gold/Creme: „PAN" auf dem rechten Cover.
- Schrift: klassische Serif in Versalien („HALLELUJA", „PAN"), weite Sperrung für den Namen, feine Sans für Untertitel.

**Entwurf**
```
# Reel Style Rules

## Canvas
- 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- Safe area: keep text out of the top 220px, the bottom 380px and the right 140px.

## Type
- Key words: elegant serif in caps (e.g. Cinzel or Cormorant Garamond), big, one per sentence.
- Support words: a clean thin sans (e.g. Inter), medium weight, small.
- Labels (name, titles): same sans, uppercase, very wide tracking.
- Max 3 words on screen. Never over my face.

## Color
- Background: near-black #0B0B0E with a soft warm glow.
- Main accent: ribbon red #B3121F (highlight #E0303D).
- Secondary accent: gold #D8B878, used sparingly for key words.
- Support accent: deep blue #1F3C88, only as brush-stroke shapes behind me.
- Text: #FFFFFF. Muted text: #B8B8C0.

## Motion
- Fast in (0.2–0.35s), hold, clean out. Ease-out on entrances.
- One idea per scene. Each scene fires on its trigger word.
- Use text behind me (cut-out) for hooks and big words.
- Red ribbon wipe or brush-stroke as scene transition. Subtle push-ins (max 6%), no shaky zooms.

## Sound
- Hit on the hook, tap on clicks, soft whoosh on scene changes, a warm chime to close.
- Voice and music always clean; voice on top. Master to -14 LUFS, peaks under -1 dB.

## Don't
- No white sticker outline around me.
- No emoji captions, no rainbow colors, max 2 fonts on screen.
- Red only as accent, never as full background.
```
